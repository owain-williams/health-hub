# Q&A

Questions a Person asks about their own Facts, answered only from cited Facts.

## Language

**Question**:
A query from a Person, asked against that Person's Facts.
_Avoid_: Prompt, message, chat

**Answer**:
A response built only from Facts. Every claim cites the Fact it rests on and shows whether that Fact came from a document or is the Person's Account. When the Facts do not contain an answer, or only Person Statements do, the Answer says so plainly.
_Avoid_: Response, completion, reply

**Declined**:
An Answer to a question that asks for interpretation or advice. It restates the relevant Facts with citations and says plainly that Health Hub does not interpret or advise.
_Avoid_: Refusal, safety response

**Conversation**:
A persisted sequence of Questions and Answers a Person can return to.
_Avoid_: Chat, session, thread
